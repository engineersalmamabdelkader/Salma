The 4-part structure

Turn a racing mind into a clear message.
1
IntroductionSay your main point first.
2
Main point 1Explain your first idea.
3
Main point 2Add a reason or example.
4
ConclusionWrap up in one sentence.

Example

Talk about a project you worked on.

  1. What was it?
  2. What did you do?
  3. What was challenging?
  4. What did you learn?

Anti-freeze protocol

When your mind goes blank, you don't need the perfect answer.
1
Buy 3 seconds“Let me think about that for a second.”
2
HeadlineGive your current best answer in one sentence.
3
SupportAdd one reason, example or next step.
4
StopDon't keep talking just because silence feels uncomfortable.
Remember: confidence is often a behavior you perform before it becomes a feeling.
🌿

90-second mind reset

For overthinking, embarrassment, tension or the urge to avoid.
01 · Name it

“I feel activated. That does not mean something is wrong.”

02 · Separate facts from story

Fact: what actually happened? Story: what am I predicting people think about me?

03 · Choose the next tiny action

Send the message. Ask the question. Speak again. Move your body. Continue.

Your rules under pressure

Simple behaviors that make you harder to derail.
1
Don't defend your worthSolve the situation. You do not need to prove you deserve a seat at the table.
2
One reason is enoughOver-explaining can turn a clear boundary into a negotiation you didn't ask for.
3
Alternative, not apology“I can do X by Thursday” is stronger than a long apology for not doing Y today.
4
Pause after the askLet the other person respond. Silence is not failure.
